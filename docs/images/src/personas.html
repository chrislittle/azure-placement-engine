Who does what

The application team fills in a form. They do not write YAML and they do not have access to the platform repository. Everything between the form and the handover belongs to the platform team. The workload team picks it up afterwards and deploys.

The flow, by who owns each part
Application team No Azure rights needed. No repository access.
1. Intake form
Region, how many vCPUs, what kind of workload, does it need to survive a datacentre failure, prod or devtest. Nobody is asked to name a VM family.

Platform team Owns the form, both YAML files and every pipeline.
2. Request pipeline
Turns the form into requests/<app>.yaml in the platform repository. The application team never writes this.
3. Pull request
A person reviews the generated request. The plan later shows the chosen family and the quota to be written, before anything is written.
rules.yaml
One file for the whole platform, hand-written. Allowlists, denylists, a vCPU cap, and which family to prefer. Enforced in the pipeline, which is the only place it can be enforced.
The pipeline Both stages, one after the other.

Workload team After the handover. Reader on their own subscription is enough.
7. They are told what they got
The family, the sizes inside it that fit, and how many of each the request needs.
8. They can ask again, any time
examples/what-can-i-deploy, Terraform or PowerShell, reads only. A value written down at vending time is a snapshot; this asks the subscription as it is now.

Repository access None required. Owns it.
Azure rights None required. Optionally Reader on their own subscription. Reader and Quota Request Operator.
Receives A subscription with quota, the family, and the sizes inside it that fit. The decision, and why each family lost.
A rule cannot be enforced by a script the workload team runs, because they would simply not pass the file. Rules are enforced in the pipeline, where the platform team controls which rules file is used. By the time the workload team reads their quota, the rules have already decided what they were granted.
