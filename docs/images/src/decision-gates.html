Reading a decision

Four gates, in this order. A gate means nothing until the gates above it pass. Quota and access are separate things, and quota for a family the subscription cannot deploy is useless — so access is checked first. A business rule can reject the request before any gate runs.

What each status means, and what to do
statusMeaningWhat to do
satisfied Existing quota covers it. Nothing. writes_required is empty.
needs_allocation A quota group covers the shortfall. Apply. Allocation is self-service and succeeds. Needs a quota group, which is not built yet.
needs_increase The regional cap has to be raised. Apply, but this is not a promise. Increases are evaluated, not granted.
not_ready Microsoft.Compute is not registered yet. Wait and retry. Transient, and normal right after vending.
blocked_by_region The subscription cannot reach the region at all. Region access request. No quota action helps.
blocked_by_lifecycle Every candidate is under the July 2026 capacity growth restrictions. Use a successor family. The reason names them.
blocked_by_access No candidate can deploy here. Read access.remediation. It names the right ticket, or says there isn't one.
blocked_by_rule A business rule refused it, before any gate ran. Change the request or the rule.
infeasible No candidate family can reach the requested size. Different region, family, or size.
The fields worth reading after status
considered
Every candidate with its numbers and why it lost. "Why not that family" is most of what anyone actually asks.
sizes
What to actually deploy, largest first. A family cannot be deployed; a size can. A size the quota cannot run is never listed.
access.remediation
Names the specific ticket: region or SKU access, zonal enablement, or none at all when the offer excludes the SKU.
regional
The region-wide vCPU cap, shared by every family. It binds separately from the family limit and is raised separately.
A refused quota write fails the apply on purpose. Terraform cannot catch a resource error, and it is the right outcome anyway: a vended subscription that cannot run its workload is a failure, not a caveat. Three refusal codes, none retryable — ContactSupport, QuotaNotAvailableForResource, DeprecatedQuotaType. PreconditionFailed is not one of them: it comes from polling, and has been seen on a write Azure completed.
