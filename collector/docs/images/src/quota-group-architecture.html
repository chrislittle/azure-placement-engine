Where a quota group sits in Azure Quota Vending

Stage 1 creates the subscription. Stage 2 gives it quota. Today that quota can only come from what the subscription already holds, so a request above its limit is refused. The quota group is the pool stage 2 is meant to draw from, and it is not built yet — everything dashed and orange below is the work this collector exists to make possible.

The pool not built

Holds the group object. Membership is not inherited from the hierarchy, so this is an access-control choice and nothing else.

Why a pool and not a support request. Allocating from a group needs no Microsoft involvement and succeeds. Asking Azure to raise a per-subscription limit is evaluated, often refused, and cannot be the thing a vending pipeline depends on. That is why AQV does not ask.

What it takes to operate
GroupQuota Request Operator
e2217c0e-04bb-4724-9580-91cf9871bc01
Carries the group operations and Microsoft.Quota/quotas/write, so it covers both halves of the write path. No Microsoft.Support/*.
Eligibility
EA, MCA-Enterprise or Internal billing account. IaaS compute only, public cloud only. A subscription belongs to one group at a time.
Enforcement unknown
The aggregate usage read refuses on an unenforced group. Nothing Microsoft publishes explains what enforcement is. The collector's first job.
A quota group solves quota. It does not solve access: a subscription without regional or zonal access fails both the allocation and the deployment, however many cores are pointed at it. That is why aqv-decide checks access before quota and will keep doing so.
